It speaks Canadian.

Most budgeting apps are built for American households and quietly assume American accounts. WiseNest was built here, for the money a Canadian family actually deals with.

Registered accounts

RRSPs, TFSAs and RESPs sit alongside everything else

Track balances month to month and watch the trend, without pretending a registered account is just another savings account.

Benefits

The CCB lands on the calendar like any other pay

Child benefit, pension, part-time shifts, irregular hours. Income that varies is the normal case, not an edge case.

Side income

Your numbers never touch our servers.

WiseNest doesn't connect to your bank, and it doesn't hold your data. You type your money in, and it syncs to a single file in your own Dropbox. We can't read it, because we never have it.

That also means there's no storage bill to pass on to you — which is why WiseNest won't ever be a monthly subscription.

What's actually in it.

No dashboards for the sake of dashboards. Every part of WiseNest exists because a household needed to answer a specific question.

Money out

Every bill on the day it leaves

Set a payment date once and it appears on the calendar every month. Bills that fall on several days, or at the end of the month, are handled properly rather than averaged away.

Money in

Pay that doesn't arrive monthly

Weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly or one-off. Override a single paycheque when the hours were different, without disturbing the rest of the year.

Chequing

What the balance will be, not what it was

WiseNest projects your account forward through every payment and paycheque, so you can see the low point coming while there's still time to move something.

Debt

A payoff date, not just a balance

Mortgage, car, lines of credit and cards, each with its own rate and payment, projected out so you can see what an extra hundred a month actually buys you.

Credit cards

Statement periods, not calendar months

Track spending against the actual statement cycle, keep an eye on what's owed versus what's been charged since, and log the cash back you've earned.

Investments

Balances month over month

Record what each account is worth and watch the trend build. Registered or not, all of it counts toward the net worth figure on the dashboard.

Side business

Books that stay separate

Bookings, expenses and profit for work on the side. Revenue counts when the money actually arrives, not when the job was quoted, so the numbers match your bank.

Everywhere

Phone, tablet and desktop

Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app. It keeps working with no signal, and catches up the next time you're online.
