Your numbers never touch our servers.

WiseNest doesn't connect to your bank, and it doesn't hold your data. You type your money in, and it syncs to a single file in your own Dropbox. We can't read it, because we never have it.

That also means no monthly fee for storage we're not paying for. Buy it once.

Where a WiseNest budget lives

Your phone — the app, working offline
Your Dropbox — one file, in a folder only WiseNest can see
Your other devices — the same file, kept in step
Our servers — nothing

Built at a kitchen table, not a startup.

I'm a licensed practical nurse in Mount Pearl. I built WiseNest because my wife and I needed to know what was actually left between paycheques — with a mortgage, kids, and her face-painting business running alongside it all.

It got good enough that people started asking for a copy. Everything in it is there because our household needed it, which is a slower way to build an app and, I think, a better one.
